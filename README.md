"The World in One Image" is a front-page poster that sums up the world news of Saturday, 21 November 2026, in a single 1024×1024 image. Five stories, each kept to the facts as reported that day, fill the page: the COP31 climate talks in Antalya running into overtime after fossil fuels vanished from the draft deal, Saudi air defences intercepting three ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh, a strong earthquake in central Peru felt in Lima, the FBI investigating a suspected hack of its national crime database, and a third Salvadoran soldier dying in Haiti after an explosion at camp. The date is printed large across the masthead, every story pairs a short headline and plain summary with a drawn illustration, and all text is rendered as crisp HTML text — no words inside generated images.
